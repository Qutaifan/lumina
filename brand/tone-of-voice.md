# Lumina Tone of Voice

We are: confident, discreet, authoritative, warm
We are not: pushy, generic, corporate, flashy

Principles:
- Speak to the buyer as a peer, not a prospect
- Less is more — remove every unnecessary word
- Never use: "amazing deal", "best price", "don't miss out"
- Always use: specific facts, quiet confidence, lifestyle language

Examples:
BAD: "Amazing 3BR apartment available now! Best price in Abdoun!"
GOOD: "A 3-bedroom apartment in Abdoun. Quiet floor, city views, private parking."

Arabic tone: formal but warm. Gulf-appropriate.
