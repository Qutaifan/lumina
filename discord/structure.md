# Lumina HQ — Discord Server Structure

## Server: Lumina HQ

---

CATEGORY: COMMAND
  #daily-brief       — morning brief, yesterday recap, today's priorities
  #decisions         — decisions made, logged with date and reason

CATEGORY: LEADS
  #new-leads         — every new inquiry logged (name, source, type, budget)
  #hot-leads         — HOT leads only, daily follow-up tracked here
  #viewing-schedule  — confirmed viewings with time, property, buyer
  #closed-deals      — every closed deal logged here (property, value, commission)

CATEGORY: PROPERTIES
  #new-listings      — new properties added to inventory
  #price-changes     — price updates on active listings
  #pipeline          — properties under evaluation (not yet listed)

CATEGORY: MARKETING
  #content-calendar  — weekly Instagram + WhatsApp content plan
  #posted-today      — links to content posted today
  #ideas             — content ideas, campaigns, angles

CATEGORY: OPERATIONS
  #tasks             — daily action items
  #weekly-review     — Sunday performance summary
  #documents         — links to master plan, trackers, templates

CATEGORY: PRIVATE
  #financials        — commission pipeline, revenue tracking
  #legal             — contract notes, MOU status
  #notes             — private strategy notes

---

## Channel Discipline

- Every lead that comes in → logged in #new-leads within 1 hour
- Every deal that closes → logged in #closed-deals same day
- #daily-brief posted every morning by 9:00 AM
- #weekly-review posted every Sunday by 18:00
