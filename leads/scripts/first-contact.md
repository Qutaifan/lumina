# First Contact Script

## WhatsApp (within 2 hours of inquiry)

---
Hello [Name],

Thank you for your interest in Lumina.

I'm Ahmad — could I ask a few quick questions to find the right properties for you?

1. What type of property are you looking for? (apartment / villa / penthouse)
2. Which areas interest you most?
3. What is your budget range?
4. Are you looking to move in or invest?
5. What is your timeline?

Looking forward to helping you find the right property.

Ahmad | Lumina Luxury Real Estate
---

## Call Script

Open: "Hello [Name], this is Ahmad from Lumina Real Estate. You inquired about [property/area]. Do you have 2 minutes?"

Qualify: Ask the 5 questions above.

Close: "I have 2–3 properties that fit what you're describing. Can I send them over on WhatsApp and arrange a private viewing?"
