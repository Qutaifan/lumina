# Lumina First Response Template

Use manually only after a visitor messages Lumina. Do not auto-send.

## First reply

Thank you for reaching out. This property is part of a curated preview set we’re currently evaluating.

May I ask what specifically interested you:
- the area
- the property type
- or investment potential?

## Follow-up classification

A) Casual curiosity
- Give general area/property insight.
- Keep tone calm and advisory.
- Do not dump full property details.

B) Serious interest
- Ask what they are looking for and preferred budget/timeline.
- Offer similar options or private conversation.
- Keep details verification-safe until owner/agent confirmation.

## Do not say

- exclusive
- guaranteed
- best deal
- limited offer
- verified unless confirmed
