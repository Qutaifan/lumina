# Lumina

> Luxury Real Estate Intelligence & Operations Platform

Lumina is a modern luxury real estate platform built to streamline property discovery, market intelligence, lead management, and high-value real estate operations.

Designed for premium markets and data-driven decision-making, Lumina combines technology, automation, and structured workflows to support brokers, investors, operators, and real estate professionals.

---

## Vision

Luxury real estate deserves better tools.

Most property websites focus only on listings.

Lumina is designed to become a complete operating system for luxury real estate.

The long-term vision is to centralize:

- Property intelligence
- Lead pipelines
- Market research
- Listing management
- Client relationships
- Digital marketing
- Business workflows

into a single platform.
