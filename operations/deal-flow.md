# Deal Flow Stages

## Stages

1. LISTING ACQUIRED — exclusive or co-broker agreement signed
2. LISTED — live on website, photos done, broadcast sent
3. VIEWINGS ACTIVE — at least 1 viewing scheduled or completed
4. OFFER RECEIVED — buyer has submitted a price
5. NEGOTIATION — back and forth between buyer and seller
6. MOU SIGNED — memorandum of understanding executed
7. DUE DILIGENCE — legal review, title check, financing confirmed
8. TRANSFER IN PROGRESS — land department process started
9. CLOSED — transfer complete, commission collected

## Timelines (typical)

Stage 1 → 2: 3–5 days (photos, copy, upload)
Stage 2 → 3: 1–4 weeks (depends on marketing)
Stage 3 → 5: 1–3 weeks
Stage 5 → 6: 2–7 days
Stage 6 → 9: 2–8 weeks (legal and land department speed)

## Red Flags to Watch

- Owner who won't drop price after 30+ days with no offers
- Buyer who views but never makes an offer after 2 viewings
- Legal issues surfaced during due diligence
- Financing falling through after MOU
